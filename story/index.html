Behind the scenes

The story.

Three browser game engines, one AI collaboration, zero installs.

One person. Three engines. Built with AI.

Every world you see on this hub was coded with AI as a co-pilot. Starting from a blank file, describing the vision in plain sentences — then iterating line-by-line until something playable shipped. No game engine, no Unity, no Unreal. Just vanilla HTML, Canvas, WebGL, and a browser.

"The barrier to making games has collapsed. If you can describe it, you can build it."

Three projects, three vastly different genres:

  • CraftVerse — a voxel sandbox with infinite terrain, biome blending, and playground creativity. Minecraft-compatible, built from scratch.
  • Mindcraft — an AI agent launcher that lets a local LLM control bots in a world, making decisions in real time.
  • DeadTakeover Protocol — a full zombie survival FPS with twelve hand-crafted maps, loot tables, horde escalation, and squad matchmaking. All browser-native.
"From concept to prototype inside a single chat session. That's the new normal."

Why a hub?

Each engine was originally a standalone project living in its own repo. As the collection grew, it needed a single front door — a place where anyone could browse, read about, and play each one without cloning repos or installing runtimes.

This site is that door. The Play page loads each game inside its own iframe from its natural build output, so what you see in the player is the real thing. The Showcase page is a gallery of what shipped, what held up, and what broke (and how it got fixed).

"Building games with AI is 10x faster than the old workflow — but 100x more important to understand what the AI is putting in the code."

What's next.

More maps, better AI, improved performance on low-end hardware, and deeper multiplayer hooks. This hub grows as each engine grows.
