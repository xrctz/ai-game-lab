MIDNIGHT WATCH

Bruno's Family Pizzeria

Check the cameras. Close the doors. Mind the power.
Survive until 6 AM.

Original fan-inspired horror game. Not affiliated with Five Nights at Freddy's. Characters & map built in Blender.

How to Play

  • A / ← — Look left (left door)
  • D / → — Look right (right door)
  • S / ↓ — Look center (desk)
  • Q — Toggle left door
  • E — Toggle right door
  • Z — Hold left hall light (peek!)
  • C — Hold right hall light
  • Map dots = animatronics. Cam 5 kitchen may be audio-only.
  • Closing cameras briefly locks doors — be ready.
  • Space / Ctrl — Open / close camera tablet
  • On cams: drag or ←→↑↓ to look around
  • I IR night vision · F flashlight · R center view
  • M — Mute / unmute audio
  • Click camera buttons on the map to switch feeds
  • Doors, lights, IR & flash drain power. Use sparingly.
  • If an animatronic reaches an open door… game over.
